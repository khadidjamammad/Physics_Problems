# 2. Resistors

You have exactly three 1 Ω resistors.

## Case 1: All in Series

Req = 1 + 1 + 1 = 3 Ω

---

## Case 2: All in Parallel

1/Req = 1 + 1 + 1 = 3

Req = 1/3 Ω

---

## Case 3: Two in Parallel, Then One in Series

Two parallel resistors:

Req = (1 × 1) / (1 + 1)

Req = 1/2 Ω

Now add the third resistor in series:

Req = 1/2 + 1 = 3/2 Ω

Req = 1.5 Ω

---

## Case 4: Two in Series, Then One in Parallel

Two series resistors:

Req = 1 + 1 = 2 Ω

Now place in parallel with the third resistor:

Req = (2 × 1) / (2 + 1)

Req = 2/3 Ω

---

# Final Unique Equivalent Resistances

- 3 Ω
- 1/3 Ω
- 3/2 Ω (1.5 Ω)
- 2/3 Ω
