# 3. Mixed Circuit

All resistors have resistance 5 Ω.

## Step 1: Identify Series Branches

### Left branch

Two resistors in series:

Req = 5 + 5 = 10 Ω

---

### Middle branch

Three resistors in series:

Req = 5 + 5 + 5 = 15 Ω

---

### Right branch

Two resistors in series:

Req = 5 + 5 = 10 Ω

---

## Step 2: Combine Parallel Branches

Middle and right branches are in parallel:

Req = (15 × 10) / (15 + 10)

Req = 150 / 25

Req = 6 Ω

---

## Step 3: Add Remaining Series Resistance

Total resistance:

Req = 10 + 6

Req = 16 Ω

---

# Final Answer

Equivalent Resistance = 16 Ω
