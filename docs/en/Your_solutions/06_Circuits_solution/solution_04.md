# 4. Mixed Circuit

All resistors have resistance 10 Ω.

## Step 1: Top Branch

Two resistors are in series:

Req = 10 + 10 = 20 Ω

---

## Step 2: Bottom Branch

The two lower resistors are in parallel:

Req = (10 × 10) / (10 + 10)

Req = 100 / 20

Req = 5 Ω

Now add the left resistor in series:

Req = 10 + 5

Req = 15 Ω

---

## Step 3: Combine Top and Bottom Branches

The 20 Ω and 15 Ω branches are in parallel:

Req = (20 × 15) / (20 + 15)

Req = 300 / 35

Req = 60 / 7 Ω

Req ≈ 8.57 Ω

---

## Step 4: Add Final Series Resistor

The last 10 Ω resistor is in series:

Req = 60/7 + 10

Req = 130/7 Ω

Req ≈ 18.57 Ω

---

# Final Answer

Equivalent Resistance ≈ 18.6 Ω
