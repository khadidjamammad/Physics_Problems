# 7. Capacitors in Parallel

Given:

- C1 = 4 μF
- C2 = 6 μF
- V = 10 V

---

## Step 1: Find Equivalent Capacitance

For capacitors in parallel:

Ceq = C1 + C2

Ceq = 4 + 6

Ceq = 10 μF

---

## Step 2: Find Charge Stored

Use:

Q = CV

Convert capacitance:

C = 10 μF = 10 × 10^-6 F

Now calculate:

Q = (10 × 10^-6)(10)

Q = 1 × 10^-4 C

Q = 100 μC

---

## Step 3: Find Energy Stored

Use:

U = (1/2)CV²

U = (1/2)(10 × 10^-6)(10²)

U = (1/2)(10 × 10^-6)(100)

U = 5 × 10^-4 J

U = 0.5 mJ

---

# Final Answers

- Equivalent Capacitance = 10 μF
- Charge Stored = 100 μC
- Energy Stored = 0.5 mJ
