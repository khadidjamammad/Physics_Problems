# 9. Current

Given:

Q(t) = 5t² + 5

---

## Step 1: Use the Definition of Current

Current is the rate of change of charge:

I(t) = dQ/dt

Differentiate Q(t):

I(t) = d/dt (5t² + 5)

I(t) = 10t

---

## Step 2: Find Current at t = 3 s

I = 10(3)

I = 30 A

---

# Final Answer

Current at t = 3 s is 30 A
