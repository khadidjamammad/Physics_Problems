# 11. Power and Energy

Given:

- R = 100 Ω
- V = 50 V

---

## Step 1: Find Power

Use the power formula:

P = V² / R

Substitute the values:

P = 50² / 100

P = 2500 / 100

P = 25 W

---

## Step 2: Convert Time

5 minutes = 300 s

---

## Step 3: Find Energy

Use:

E = Pt

Substitute the values:

E = 25 × 300

E = 7500 J

---

# Final Answers

- Power = 25 W
- Energy = 7500 J
