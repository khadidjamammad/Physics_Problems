# 13. Transformer Ratio

Given:

- Primary voltage, Vp = 120 V
- Secondary voltage, Vs = 9 V
- Number of primary turns, Np = 400

---

## Step 1: Use the Transformer Equation

Vs / Vp = Ns / Np

Substitute the values:

9 / 120 = Ns / 400

---

## Step 2: Solve for Ns

Ns = 400 × (9 / 120)

Ns = 30

---

# Final Answer

Number of secondary turns = 30 turns
