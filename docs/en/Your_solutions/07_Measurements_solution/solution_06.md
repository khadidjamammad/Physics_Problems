# Instrument Precision Measurement

## Theory

The thermometer reading is:

25.4 °C

The smallest displayed increment is:

0.1 °C

When the uncertainty is assumed to be half of the last digit:

ΔT = (0.1 °C) / 2

---

## Calculation

Absolute uncertainty:

ΔT = 0.05 °C

So, the temperature measurement is reported as:

T = 25.4 ± 0.05 °C

---

## Result

**Absolute Uncertainty:**

± 0.05 °C

**Final Measurement:**

25.4 ± 0.05 °C

25.395 - 25.405
