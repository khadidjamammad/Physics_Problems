# Standard Deviation of Test Scores

## Given Data

The test scores are:

88, 92, 79, 85, 95, 81, 86, 90, 83, 77, 89

Number of scores:

N = 11

---

## Formula for the Mean

The mean (average) is calculated using:

x̄ = (Σxi) / N

where:

- x̄ = mean
- xi = individual score
- N = number of scores

### Calculation

Sum of all scores:

88 + 92 + 79 + 85 + 95 + 81 + 86 + 90 + 83 + 77 + 89 = 945

Mean:

x̄ = 945 / 11

x̄ = 85.91

---

## Formula for Standard Deviation

The sample standard deviation is:

σ = √[ Σ(xi − x̄)² / (N − 1) ]

where:

- σ = standard deviation
- xi = individual score
- x̄ = mean
- N = number of scores

### Calculation

Using x̄ = 85.91:

Σ(xi − x̄)² = 370.91

σ = √(370.91 / 10)

σ = √37.09

σ ≈ 6.09

---

## Results for Original Data

| Statistic | Value |
|------------|--------|
| Mean | 85.91 |
| Standard Deviation | 6.09 |

---

## Remove the Highest and Lowest Scores

Highest score:

95

Lowest score:

77

Remaining scores:

88, 92, 79, 85, 81, 86, 90, 83, 89

Number of scores:

N = 9

---

## New Mean

Sum of remaining scores:

773

Mean:

x̄ = 773 / 9

x̄ = 85.89

---

## New Standard Deviation

Using x̄ = 85.89:

Σ(xi − x̄)² = 148.89

σ = √(148.89 / 8)

σ = √18.61

σ ≈ 4.31

---

## Results After Removing Outliers

| Statistic | Value |
|------------|--------|
| Mean | 85.89 |
| Standard Deviation | 4.31 |

---

## Conclusion

The original set of scores has a mean of **85.91** and a standard deviation of **6.09**.

After removing the highest score (95) and the lowest score (77), the mean changes only slightly to **85.89**, while the standard deviation decreases to **4.31**.

This shows that the highest and lowest scores increase the spread of the data. Once they are removed, the remaining scores are clustered more closely around the average.
